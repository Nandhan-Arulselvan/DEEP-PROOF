DOCUMENT INTEGRITY PLATFORM

Evidence should be verifiable.

DeepProof records a PDF fingerprint, its IPFS reference, and optional blockchain evidence—without placing the PDF itself on-chain.

BYTE-LEVEL CONFIDENCE

One changed byte creates a new SHA-256 fingerprint.

Matches establish that two PDF byte streams are identical. They do not judge a document’s truthfulness or authorship.
